About Me

Joseph Shipley is a music producer from central Florida. Has been doing music since 2020 combining synthwave and his own genres, Joseph’s goal is to put out as much music as possible mainly for fun, he is working on his first OFFICIAL Album which will come out around January-June 2027, for the time being, there are other music projects Such As BlueSkyCatMusic The Second Longterm project, and BlueFly (an upcoming experimental music project)
